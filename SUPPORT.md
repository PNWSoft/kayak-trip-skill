# Kayak Trip Planner: Support

**No support is provided.** Kayak Trip Planner is free, open-source software, provided "as is" under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) and the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md). There is no help desk, no response-time commitment and no guarantee that anything will be fixed or changed.

## Feedback and contributions
You're welcome to open an [issue](https://github.com/PNWSoft/kayak-trip-skill/issues) or a pull request on GitHub with bug reports, requests or improvements, such as regional notes for a stretch of coast you know (see Contributing in the [README](README.md#contributing)). The authors may read them, and may or may not act on them. There is no promise of a reply.

## Not for emergencies
Nothing here is monitored for safety issues on the water. In an emergency, contact the U.S. Coast Guard (VHF channel 16) or call 911.

## Problems with the data itself
Tide, current, forecast and buoy data come from NOAA, the National Weather Service and other public sources. The Tool's authors don't control them; report data problems to those agencies.
