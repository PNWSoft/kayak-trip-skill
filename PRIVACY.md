# Kayak Trip Planner: Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

**The authors of Kayak Trip Planner (the "Tool") collect no data.** The Tool has no servers, accounts, analytics, tracking or telemetry, and nothing you type or that the Tool produces is sent to its authors.

## What happens to information when you use the Tool

- **Your conversation with Claude** is handled by Anthropic, under [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), like any other Claude conversation. The Tool doesn't change that.
- **Public data sources.** To build a plan, the Tool requests data from public services: NOAA Tides & Currents and its model server (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov, api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov, opendap.co-ops.nos.noaa.gov), the National Weather Service (forecast.weather.gov, api.weather.gov), the National Data Buoy Center (ndbc.noaa.gov) and the University of British Columbia's SalishSeaCast server (salishsea.eos.ubc.ca). Those requests contain the coordinates of your launch and nearby water, dates and station IDs, and nothing else about you. Like any website, those services may log requests under their own policies.
- **Claude's memory.** If you have memory turned on, Claude may save your acceptance of the [Terms](TERMS.md) and your paddling profile (speed, experience level, gear) so it doesn't ask again. That memory belongs to your Claude account; you can view and delete it there.
- **Files on your computer.** In Claude Code the Tool's scripts read their bundled data and write nothing outside your normal session.

## Contact
The Tool is unsupported (see [Support](SUPPORT.md)). Privacy questions can be raised as an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/PNWSoft/kayak-trip-skill/issues); don't post personal information there.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published in this repository with a new effective date.
