## 1. What the Tool is and isn't
The Tool assembles publicly available data, such as tide and current predictions, model forecasts and marine forecasts from NOAA and the National Weather Service, and compares it with published reference conditions to suggest days, times and routes for paddling. It is a planning tool, not a safety system. Output is general information only. It is not professional advice, instruction, guiding or a safety assessment, and it is not a substitute for training, experience, local knowledge or your own judgment. It never tells you a day is safe.

## 2. Limitations of the data and the Tool
Predictions and forecasts can be wrong, out of date, incomplete or unavailable. Stations and models may be far from your route, and conditions can differ greatly over short distances. The Tool does not observe real conditions, does not know your abilities, equipment or health, and does not know about local hazards, closures, traffic or regulations. The Tool uses artificial intelligence, which can make mistakes, including errors in reading data, calculations or reasoning. Check information yourself before relying on it.

## 3. Risk
Paddling and other water activities are inherently dangerous and can result in property damage, serious injury or death, including from cold water, wind, waves, currents, fog, weather, collisions and equipment failure. You take on those risks when you paddle, whether or not you used the Tool to plan.

## 4. Your responsibility
You are responsible for deciding whether, when and where to paddle; for your own safety and the safety of anyone paddling with you or relying on a plan you share; for checking conditions yourself before and during any trip; and for following applicable laws and regulations. If you share Output with others, make sure they understand this notice. Every plan carries its own "Not covered by this plan" section for that reason.
