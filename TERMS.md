## 1. What the Tool is and isn't
The Tool reports estimated conditions from publicly available third-party data, such as tide and current predictions, model forecasts and marine forecasts from NOAA and the National Weather Service, and compares them with published reference conditions to suggest days, times and routes that match the conditions you ask about. It is a planning tool, not a safety system. Output is general information only. It is not professional advice, instruction, guiding or a safety assessment, and it is not a substitute for training, experience, local knowledge or your own judgment. It never says that anything is safe or safer.

## 2. Limitations of the data and the Tool
Predictions and forecasts can be wrong, out of date, incomplete or unavailable. Stations and models may be far from your route, and conditions can differ greatly over short distances. The Tool does not observe real conditions, does not know your abilities, equipment or health, and does not know about local hazards, closures, traffic or regulations. The Tool uses artificial intelligence, which can make mistakes, including errors in reading data, calculations or reasoning. Check information yourself before relying on it.
